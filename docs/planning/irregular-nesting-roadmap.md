# Irregular Nesting Roadmap

This is the active forward roadmap for convex irregular nesting after the
intertwined Compact promotion. It contains only current behavior, standing
promotion gates, and uncompleted work. Completed and rejected experiments live
under [`../history/`](../history/README.md) and
[`../research/`](../research/index.md).

## Current Production Boundary

The Compact quality profile runs one deterministic anytime coordinator around
two protected cohorts:

- sheetless complete constructors submit canonical-exact endpoints to the
  complete namespace and retain their existing intrinsic comparator;
- resumable cold and warm capacity lanes submit exact subset endpoints to a
  separate count/material-first namespace;
- a fitting settled complete endpoint dominates every capacity endpoint;
- after a complete miss, the existing capacity checkpoints continue without
  restarting;
- requested-sheet dimensions affect exact capacity legality and q0/q90 endpoint
  fit, never complete construction, survivor slots, or intrinsic ranking.

The coordinator currently advances a four-depth cold-capacity checkpoint before
interleaving piece-boundary complete work with capacity resumes. This preserves
work and chronology on one worker; it is not parallel execution, so protected
CPU costs can still add.

The ordinary requested-sheet decoder remains available when Compact is not
eligible, including intentional `short_side_fill` and active GA configurations.

## Standing Promotion Gate

Every search-quality, scheduling, or performance experiment must preserve:

- exact canonical legality and complete placed/unplaced accounting;
- deterministic pause/resume and scheduler chronology;
- the protected production fallback for all nine accepted cases;
- exact roomy complete hashes, source selection, archive order, and q0/q90 fit;
- separate complete and capacity survivor lists, budgets, and comparators;
- complete-over-capacity terminal dominance;
- the user-owned `sortPiecesForNesting` behavior;
- committed source plus request/settings, evaluations, elapsed and CPU time,
  peak memory, trace, SVG, PNG, report, and checksums.

Promotion needs two clean reproductions from one committed source. A deadline,
evaluation cap, or incomplete source set must remain explicit and cannot be
reported as proof of infeasibility or settled completeness.

## Active Priorities

### Latest completed quality checkpoint

The bounded focused complete reconstruction is the latest accepted checkpoint.
It rebuilds only the settled sheetless leader under the generic
`endpoint-q90-right-to-left` order, then submits the result to the unchanged
complete archive. Shapes-17 improves from `304,499.845650` to
`281,233.148068 mm2`, from ten to four isolated pieces, and from `559.975` to
`532.691 mm` maximum side. It keeps `17/17` pieces and zero cavities. The
focused stage uses `8,035` evaluations and about `1.7 s`; exact-preflight and
no-fitting-complete cases skip it with zero work. See
[`../research/focused-complete-reconstruction.md`](../research/focused-complete-reconstruction.md).

The earlier singleton reinsertion observer remains closed and rejected. It is
historical evidence that local one-piece repair could not reach this result.

### P0: One-count-deficit capacity observer

Hypothesis: on Mixed-61 `300 x 300`, a useful large-piece state is generated but
discarded because all 16 cohesion slots remain at the current best placed
count. Preserving one state exactly one piece behind may let it accept later
small pieces and recover.

Protected scope:

- create an independent subset-only observer lane;
- compute the exact unchanged width-16 cohesion-retained list first;
- when a distinct candidate with
  `placedCount = depthBestCount - 1` exists, replace only the deterministic
  sixteenth control entry with the best such candidate under the existing
  material, cavity, and compactness comparator;
- when no such candidate exists, retain the complete unchanged width-16 list;
- reuse the existing successors, contact fanout, legality, deduplication,
  checkpoint, and evaluation cap;
- leave the production width-16 lane, complete cohort, initial order, terminal
  selector, and output unchanged.

Measurement: run Mixed-61 `300 x 300` first. Record whether a deficit state
exists before retention, its exact identity and parent, whether and where it
recovers to the leading count, later small-piece placements, endpoint objective,
evaluations, elapsed/CPU time, and memory. Run the nine-case archive and one
predeclared mixed-scale case only after a strict targeted improvement.

Falsifier: no deficit state exists; the retained state never recovers; the
endpoint remains at or below `6/61`; improvement requires new candidates, a
larger budget, or altered production behavior.

Promotion gate: exact resume equivalence, a strict improvement under the
existing terminal comparator, exact accounting and legality, unchanged
protected fallbacks for all nine cases, and bounded resource evidence.

Stop condition: if the targeted run shows no causal recovery or strict
improvement, archive the trace and remove the observer. Do not widen the beam
or add more deficit bands.

### P1: Discarded-family complete continuation observer

Status: closed and rejected on 2026-07-24. The observer found 16 distinct
alternates with negligible measured overhead. The single preregistered
depth-10 crossover continued exactly to completion in `798.724 ms` and 3,902
evaluations, but produced a worse `297,570.451566 mm2` endpoint. The accepted
`281,233.148068 mm2` Shapes endpoint remained authoritative. See
[`../research/discarded-family-complete-continuation.md`](../research/discarded-family-complete-continuation.md).

Start this only after the focused reconstruction producer has settled in
production and a construction trace shows a useful generated state disappearing
before completion.

Source correction: the production direct constructor is width one. It has no
replaceable retained survivor slot, and the bounded periodic continuations are
protected producers rather than disposable beam entries. Accepted Triangle and
Mixed winners currently depend on `periodic-P2`.

Hypothesis: the best canonically distinct non-selected transform-family winner
inside the Shapes-producing `legacy-absolute-envelope` role may expose one
useful generated future that the width-one lineage discards.

Protected scope:

- first add a zero-evaluation-delta trace with no selection or output influence;
- record at most one exact distinct non-selected family winner per depth,
  candidate/family counts, selected and alternate canonical future identity,
  and the original pending-order digest;
- cap serialized trace data at `1 MiB` and reject paired Shapes overhead above
  `500 ms`;
- do not add a basin heuristic, topology calculation, new candidate geometry,
  wider beam, periodic-slot replacement, requested-sheet feature, or
  sheet-relative score;
- only after one exact witness exists, deterministically capture its live state,
  bottom-left anchor it, verify its traced identity, and continue it from exact
  frozen placements plus the original pending suffix;
- give that one continuation a separate observer-only `12,000`-evaluation /
  `15,000 ms` cap and no production output influence.

Measurement: begin with Shapes-17 `2000 x 2700`; record the displaced control
future, alternate future, pending suffix, exact endpoint, evaluations,
elapsed/CPU time, and memory. A first win remains separately budgeted and
output-neutral through two targeted reproductions and the nine-case and
ten-sheet matrices.

Falsifier: no distinct family winner exists; trace overhead exceeds `500 ms`;
the alternate cannot be rehydrated exactly; continuation reaches its cap,
remains incomplete, duplicates an accepted endpoint, or does not strictly
improve the exact complete objective. Stop before any matrix on those outcomes.

Promotion remains a later decision. Do not borrow the accepted focused
reconstruction entitlement unless both the focused fallback and the new
continuation are proven to settle within one aggregate `12,000`-evaluation
budget. Otherwise an additional production entitlement requires explicit
acceptance.

The pinned libnest2d, Sparrow, Deepnest/SVGnest, and PackingSolver sources
support pending-aware protected diversity and bounded portfolio accounting.
They do not justify random GA mutation, overlapping infeasible states,
restart-based widening, or strip-width fitness.

Decision: do not promote or generalize this observer from the losing witness.
The exact warm-handoff mechanism remains useful evidence, but it is not a
production quality improvement. Continue with P2 corpus coverage rather than
widening this hypothesis.

### P2: Close complete-cohort invariance and corpus coverage

Status: the historical ten-sheet Mixed-61 subgate passed twice at `6179cef` on
2026-07-24 with identical geometry and portable artifacts. Keep it as a
standing gate. Broader mixed-50, rectangle, trapezoid, pentagon, star, and other
representative-fixture coverage remains open.

Hypothesis: the protected sheetless complete winner remains identical on every
roomy sheet where the same leader fits, and the nine-case gate generalizes to
the maintained corpus.

Protected scope: measurement only. Do not modify search, allocation, ranking,
candidate generation, or fit behavior.

Measurement: retain the historical roomy-sheet matrix as a standing gate with
source order, settlement, canonical hash, normalized SVG, q0/q90 fit, exact
partition, runtime, and checksums. Add maintained mixed-50, rectangle,
trapezoid, pentagon, star, and other representative fixtures to a reusable
corpus gate.

Falsifier: different complete selection or geometry where the same leader fits;
nondeterministic source order; censored work reported as settled; or a
render/report mismatch.

Promotion gate: two clean reproductions, portable artifacts, exact existing
goldens, and documented fit-boundary explanations for legitimate divergence.

Stop condition: once reproducible, make the matrix and corpus a standing gate
and remove them from active work.

### P3: Conditional large-first/small-fill subset observer

Start this only if an uncensored P0 trace proves a causal ordering loss under
the immutable prepared-order pass: a competitive macro lineage must have
irrevocably processed a small-tier piece before the required macro state became
available. Do not start P3 merely because P0 has no eligible deficit witness,
loses terminally, or is censored.

Hypothesis: a bounded two-phase decision schedule can protect a legal macro
arrangement and then fill its remaining space with smaller pending pieces.

Protected scope: use a separate subset-only observer with no output influence.
Do not change `sortPiecesForNesting`. Derive a producer-local large/small
partition from exact convex-hull area, preserve relative prepared order inside
each tier, test one preregistered dimensionless split such as PackingSolver's
`maxArea / 8`, and use no more than one ordinary capacity entitlement.

Measurement: Mixed-61 `300 x 300`, one second mixed-scale fixture, and only
after a targeted gain the nine-case gate. Record deferred/pending partitions,
phase budgets, count recovery, evaluations, and whether improvement comes from
ordering rather than extra work.

Falsifier: P0 already recovers the opportunity; no strict targeted improvement;
fixture-specific logic, new candidate generation, excess budget, or a future
decision state that cannot resume exactly.

Promotion gate: strict predeclared mixed-scale improvement, deterministic
resume, unchanged production cohorts, nine-case fallback, and exact phase
accounting.

Stop condition: reject after the one bounded rule. Do not proceed directly to
GA order mutation.

### P4: Parallel protected-cohort wall-time experiment

This is a separate performance track, not a search-quality mechanism.

Hypothesis: independently executing complete and capacity work can reduce
elapsed time on expensive complete misses while retaining deterministic reuse
and complete dominance.

Protected scope: give each cohort private state and caches, merge endpoints
deterministically, cancel capacity after a fitting complete settlement, and
retain the live capacity checkpoint/result after a complete miss. Do not alter
search order, comparators, survivor lists, or CPU entitlements.

Measurement: use one roomy fitting case, Mixed-61 `700 x 500` as the historical
expensive miss, and one exact-preflight bypass. Compare repeated wall time,
user CPU, peak RSS, hashes, checkpoint ledgers, cancellation latency, and join
order against current single-worker production.

Falsifier: no preregistered wall-time reduction; restarted/lost capacity work;
output dependent on task completion order; changed complete dominance; or
resource growth beyond the declared operational budget.

Promotion gate: the exact nine-case archive, deterministic repeated results,
failure/deadline/cancellation coverage, and a meaningful paired wall-time gain
on the expensive-miss case. Require exact ledger equality for settled-miss and
exact-preflight cases. On fitting cases, preserve endpoint identity and require
the timing-dependent capacity ledger to be internally reconciled, monotonic,
restart-free, and free of work after cancellation acknowledgement.

Stop condition: stop after the three-case measurement if concurrency only
redistributes chronology or increases operational cost.

## Open-Source Mechanism Disposition

The locally pinned sources under `knowledge/dependencies/` are controls, not
dependencies:

- Deepnest and SVGnest justify bounded outer order/rotation diversity, but
  their default GA semantics do not replace the protected complete cohort or
  the user-owned order boundary.
- libnest2d's pending-aware placement hook informs the protected future-state
  classification in P1. Its per-rotation coverage is already represented by
  Compact transform-family construction.
- PackingSolver's bounded portfolio accounting and deterministic parallel
  result merge inform P3/P4. Periodic cells already exist in Compact;
  large-first/small-fill remains conditional on P0 evidence.
- Sparrow supports bounded diverse samples and explicit phase budgets. Its
  overlapping infeasible search cannot enter exact Compact state or history.
- Dalsoo's leave-pending and feature-contact lessons are already represented by
  capacity skip continuation and production contact fanout. Its greedy
  feasibility and terminal score remain incompatible.
